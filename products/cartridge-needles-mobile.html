Overview of Core Manufacturing Capabilities

The core of a tattoo cartridge lies in the absolute quality and stability of the needles.

03. High-Precision Mold Manufacturing System

Before leaving the factory, all tattoo needles undergo mandatory, rigorous EO (Ethylene Oxide) sterilization to guarantee professional hygiene standards.

1. Sealed Packaging

Within a Class 100,000 cleanroom, finished products are perfectly sealed in medical-grade polymer breathable blister packs to prevent secondary contamination.

2. EO Sterilization

Placed into large-scale EO sterilization cabinets for a thorough sterilization cycle under strictly controlled concentration, temperature, and humidity.

3. Sterilization Validation

Rigorous validation using biological indicators and chemical indicator cards ensures absolute safety with a zero bacteria survival rate.

4. Batch Inspection

Each batch undergoes desorption monitoring and sterile sampling. Only after passing are they released with an independent sterilization batch number.

04. Comprehensive Medical-Grade Sterilization Process

Sealed Packaging Testing: Water bath or negative pressure sampling to ensure zero leaks in the medical-grade breathable paper seal.

05. Strict Final Quality Control

Before packaging, every batch of tattoo needles undergoes a complete final quality inspection:

Needle Tip Sharpness Inspection: Removing any barbs or blunt tips under a high-powered microscope.

Grouping Stability Testing: Simulating high-frequency penetration to ensure no needle scattering or deviation.

Housing Assembly Inspection: Manual re-inspection of structural alignment to prevent ink leakage or detachment.
