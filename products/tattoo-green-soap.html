Professional-Grade Tattoo Cleansing Foam

High-quality cleansing foam is an indispensable professional consumable during the tattooing process. Our products are designed to:

Remove Excess Pigment：Rapidly lift and remove surface residue.

Cleanse the Tattoo Area：Maintain absolute freshness and clarity of the wound site.

Reduce Skin Irritation：Minimize physical damage caused by the procedure.

Enhance Procedural Comfort：Significantly elevate the overall client experience.

A professional-grade cleansing system is the core of operational efficiency.

Our premium foam achieves:

Instant Pigment Breakdown：Dissolves residue in seconds.

Reduced Wiping Frequency：Dramatically lowers client pain and skin stress.

Ink Residue Decomposition：Prevents color cross-contamination.

Visual Clarity：Ensures the tattoo area remains clear to showcase true-to-life colors.

Efficiency means precision: Allowing artists to clearly observe every line and shading detail throughout the process.

04. Gentle Amino Acid Cleansing System

Three Core Advantages:

Gentle & Non-Irritating: The weak acidic pH is naturally compatible with human skin, showing extreme tolerance for open micro-wounds.

Sensitive Skin Friendly: Significantly reduces the risk of allergic reactions, providing the safest experience for sensitive skin.

Moisture Balance: Cleanses without stripping the lipid barrier, preventing tightness and dryness to aid in the healing process.
