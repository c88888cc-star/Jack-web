Professional-grade tattoo diluents play a crucial harmonizing role in tattoo creation, primarily used for:

Adjusting ink concentration: Precisely matching the color requirements of different styles.

Enhancing pigment dispersion: Allowing pigment powders to perfectly release within the solution.

Stabilizing ink structure: Preventing water-oil separation and deterioration.

Creating Gray Washes: An essential aid for black and grey realism styles.

High-quality diluents ensure:

Natural color transitions: Smooth gradients without harsh lines or graininess.

Even pigment distribution: Eliminating color block deposition for smoother coloring.

No impact on coloring effect: Does not weaken the saturation and color retention rate of the original ink.

No disruption to ink structure: Perfectly compatible with mainstream ink systems on the market.

Product Overview & Core Applications

Ordinary diluents are prone to pigment sedimentation after standing, affecting their usability.

Our exclusive formulation system builds a special microscopic suspension network, perfectly solving this industry pain point:

Maintains even pigment suspension: Remains consistent even after long periods of standing.

Enhances ink stability: Prevents pigment molecules from re-agglomerating.

Extends the lifespan of mixed inks: Reduces water evaporation and drying out in the ink cups.

Completely frees up hands: Allows tattoo artists to focus on their creation without frequently shaking the inks during work
